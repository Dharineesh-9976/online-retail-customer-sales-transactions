  1. Dataset Description

The data this project will use is the Online Retail dataset which provides details concerning individual sales transactions for an online retail company. This dataset is composed of records of individual transactions and may be used to evaluate performance concerning products sold, customer purchase behaviour, revenue streams and the geographical market in addition to time-based sales. The data is provided in a comma-separated values (CSV) file, the data was loaded into Python with the Pandas library. This dataset initially comprises of 541,909 records and 8 columns representing a large amount of transactional sales data for analysis. The 8 individual records within the dataset can be summed to: Invoice No, Stock Code, Description, Quantity, Invoice Date, Unit Price, Customer ID and Country. Each individually providing information about transactions within the company; Invoice No is unique identifier for a transaction and the purchase; this represents whether a transaction is either positive sales of items or a return of items previously bought. The Stock Code is the number that the item is identifiable by within the company, it is a product code so it would correspond to whether it is a chair or other furniture. A Description column is to provide a textual label for each product; this allows evaluation of the bestselling items and those which generate most revenue from sales. The quantity column shows how many of that particular stock code were bought as a result of the transaction number, so it represents the quantity that was sold of that particular product. The Invoice Date records the exact datetime an item was purchased or returned so it may be used for time-based analyses where one needs to look at income fluctuations and variations in behaviour based on monthly or daily cycles of business operation. Initially stored as an 'object' data type, the 'Invoice Date' data type was then altered to represent date and time to allow extraction and use of chronological information concerning sales activities for calculation purposes, for example, business days of the week compared to weekends. It was also used for comparing revenue changes over time. The Unit Price column shows the unit cost of each of the 541,909 sales transactions performed, by taking Quantity and multiplying it by Unit Price a figure for the revenue is achieved for each individual row. In this project Revenue was created by multiplying the Quantity column by the Unit Price column enabling comparison between individual products/items; the countries different markets performed in as well as their specific performance levels; compare times of the day when more goods are sold and compare individual consumers sales levels. The Customer ID column assigns each record with an identifying number for individual customers, except some records which do not have customer numbers and were kept for sales general analysis as they may still contain valuable details regarding product sales, quality, price levels or customer country but the business-level comparisons of customers would be performed on records containing the ID field. The Country shows from which country the customers are residing, so it enables us to perform a comparative analysis on countries to represent where more is being sold (Al-Etaibi,2025).

Data cleaning and preprocessing: As good quality of results is dependent upon quality of data. In real world datasets we often have a presence of missing values, duplicate rows, inconsistent data types and values, unusual transaction values which needs to be catered. Initially when the "Online Retail" dataset was explored a few data cleaning and preprocessing steps was performed on the dataset to be used for main business analysis. Initially the duplicates were looked upon in the raw dataset. In the raw dataset there were 541,909 entries; after a check for duplicates it was found that the transaction had duplicate entries. It had 5,268 duplicate rows which may have a huge impact over the product/country/customer performance, due to multiple calculation of sales quantity and amount. So, all the duplicate rows of the dataset were removed. After this operation the dataset had 536,641 entries. Next step was to find any discrepancies in the data types of different attributes.   1. Limitations of the Analysis

While useful information regarding Online Retail's sales were revealed from this analysis, there are some inherent limitations that should be mentioned before drawing conclusions from the results. The first obvious limitation that occurred from the beginning was the numerous empty entries. Specifically, Customer ID contain many empty. Although records with empty Customer IDs were kept for analysis in overall sales data, these records were not factored into customer spending. As a result, the customer spending results may not have accounted for all purchases from every single customer that made purchases in the Online Retail. The second limitation involved the presence of sales returns and cancelled transactions, or more directly, negative quantity. Therefore, I extracted a second set of data that contains only positive quantity as well as positive unit prices, so that to be used as the core data to analyze revenue of the business. However, this does not account for returned items (Abedi,2025).
