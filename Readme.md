## ⚙️ Project Workflow

### 1. Data Understanding

The project uses an Online Retail dataset containing transaction records with fields such as:

- Invoice number
- Product description
- Quantity
- Invoice date
- Unit price
- Customer ID
- Country

These fields were used to investigate sales performance, customer behavior, and geographical revenue distribution.

### 2. Data Preprocessing

The following data preparation steps were performed:

- Identified missing values.
- Removed records with missing product descriptions for product-level analysis.
- Retained transactions with missing CustomerID for overall sales analysis.
- Identified and removed duplicate records.
- Converted invoice dates into datetime format.
- Examined negative quantities and cancelled transactions.
- Checked invalid quantities and unit prices.
- Filtered transactions with positive quantities and unit prices for relevant sales analyses.

The report identified:

- **5,268 duplicate records**
- **9,725 transactions with negative quantities**
- **9,251 cancelled transactions**

Customer-level analysis used transactions with valid CustomerID values.

### 3. Feature Engineering

A new feature named `Revenue` was created to calculate the monetary value of each transaction line.

```python
Revenue = Quantity * UnitPrice
```

This feature supported product-level, country-level, and customer-level revenue analysis, as well as overall sales performance analysis.

Invoice dates were also used to investigate monthly and day-of-week revenue trends.

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

#### 📦 Product Revenue Analysis

The analysis compared revenue generated by products and transaction categories.

**Key findings:**

- DOTCOM POSTAGE generated the highest reported revenue of approximately **£206,248.77**.
- REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER and PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE were among the high-revenue physical products.
- Entries such as DOTCOM POSTAGE, POSTAGE, and Manual require careful interpretation because they may represent delivery charges or transaction adjustments rather than physical products.

#### 🌍 Geographical Sales Analysis

Revenue was compared across countries to understand geographical sales performance.

**Key findings:**

- The United Kingdom was the dominant market.
- Reported UK revenue was approximately **£9,001,744.09**.
- The Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, and France were notable international markets.
- International revenue was substantially lower than UK revenue.

These findings highlight the importance of the UK market and provide a starting point for evaluating international growth opportunities.

#### 📅 Monthly and Daily Revenue Analysis

The project investigated:

- Monthly revenue trends
- Revenue by day of the week
- Changes in purchasing activity
- Variation in sales performance over time

**Key observations:**

- Monthly revenue varied throughout the analyzed period.
- Revenue did not remain constant over time.
- Purchasing activity differed across days of the week.

These patterns can help businesses evaluate demand fluctuations and plan inventory, staffing, and marketing activities.

#### 👥 Customer Revenue Analysis

Customer-level analysis examined revenue contribution and purchasing behavior.

The analysis focused on:

- Customer revenue contribution
- Purchasing activity
- Identification of high-value customers
- Customer-level sales performance

**Key finding:** A relatively small number of customers contributed significantly to total revenue, highlighting the importance of understanding customer value and purchasing behavior.
